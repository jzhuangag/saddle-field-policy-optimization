# ICLR 2027 submission constraints

Authoritative sources:

- Author guide: https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines
- Official style archive: https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip

Working constraints for the initial submission:

- Abstract deadline: September 18, 2026, 11:59 p.m. AoE.
- Full-paper deadline: September 25, 2026, 11:59 p.m. AoE.
- Main text: at most nine pages for the initial submission.
- References and appendices: outside the main-text page limit, with appendices placed after the references.
- Review: double blind; author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and identifying repository links must be removed.
- Use the official ICLR 2027 style without modifying margins, font sizes, or spacing.
- Include the required statement about the use of large language models and follow the venue's reproducibility guidance.

Project-specific safeguards:

- The ICLR manuscript must be contribution-led and written as a learning/optimization paper, not as a shortened IEEE journal manuscript.
- Experimental comparisons must use the same minimax-learning backbone whenever the purpose is to isolate the policy-update rule.
- Claims will be narrowed to the evidence; experimental settings will not be tuned to manufacture universal superiority.
- Every added or materially changed citation must pass a fresh bibliographic existence and metadata check before submission.
